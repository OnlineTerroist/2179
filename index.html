01 — Where to fish

Victoria's Calamari Hotspots

Southern Calamari are found along Victoria's entire coastline, but sightings cluster around Port Phillip Bay, Western Port, and the Mornington Peninsula — all accessible from Melbourne. Each dot on the map is a confirmed sighting record, coloured by the decade it was recorded. Darker dots are more recent.

You don't need to travel far. Some of the highest-density sighting zones are within an hour's drive of the CBD.

02 — When to fish

Timing Is Everything

Calamari are not a year-round guarantee. The data tells a clear story: March dominates, accounting for more sightings than any other month. Summer through autumn (January–April) is prime time, when water temperatures are warmest and squid move inshore to feed and spawn.

Winter months (June–September) see far fewer encounters, though dedicated anglers still find them in deeper water off piers and jetties.

🗓 Best windows to target calamari

February to April at dawn or dusk, in 2–8 metres of water around seagrass beds, jetty pylons, and rock walls. Jig lures (size 2.0–3.0) in pink or orange outperform bait on most days.

03 — Victoria vs the nation

Victoria Leads the Nation

With 520 recorded sightings, Victoria accounts for more Southern Calamari observations than any other Australian state — ahead of New South Wales and South Australia. This reflects both the species' preference for the cooler waters of Bass Strait and Port Phillip Bay, and the size of Victoria's recreational fishing community.

04 — About this visualisation

Data & Methods

This visualisation combines two publicly available datasets:

  • Atlas of Living Australia — 2,077 occurrence records for Sepioteuthis australis, filtered to records with valid coordinates. DOI: 10.26197/ala.96c5b17f. Licence: CC BY 4.0.
  • Australian Bureau of Statistics — Statistical Area 2 (SA2) digital boundary files, 2021 edition (GDA2020). Used for the Melbourne base map. Licence: CC BY 4.0.

All visualisations were built with Vega-Lite v5. Geographic data was simplified using Mapshaper to reduce file size while preserving boundary accuracy. Analysis and data preparation in Python (pandas).
