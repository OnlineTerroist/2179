01 — Where to fish

Victoria's Calamari Hotspots

Southern Calamari cluster around Port Phillip Bay, Western Port, and the Mornington Peninsula — all within an hour's drive of Melbourne. Each dot represents a confirmed sighting, coloured by the decade it was recorded. Darker dots are more recent.

02 — When to fish

Timing Is Everything

The data tells a clear story: March dominates with 253 Victorian sightings — nearly five times the next busiest month. Summer through autumn is prime time, when water temperatures peak and squid move inshore to feed and spawn.

🗓 Best windows to target calamari

February to April at dawn or dusk, in 2–8 metres of water around seagrass beds, jetty pylons, and rock walls. Jig lures (size 2.0–3.0) in pink or orange outperform bait on most days.

03 — The citizen science boom

Anglers Are Changing the Data

Until recently, most calamari records came from scientific trawl surveys. Today, recreational anglers using iNaturalist are the single largest source of Victorian records — transforming what we know about where and when calamari appear.

Victorian sightings by data source. iNaturalist and the 1990s Port Phillip Bay Study dominate.

Cumulative Victorian sightings since 2000. The steep rise after 2019 marks the citizen science era.

04 — Victoria vs the nation

Victoria Leads the Nation

With 520 recorded sightings, Victoria accounts for more Southern Calamari observations than any other Australian state. This reflects the species' preference for the cooler waters of Bass Strait and Port Phillip Bay, and the size of Victoria's recreational fishing community.

06 — About this visualisation

Data & Methods

This visualisation combines two publicly available datasets:

  • Atlas of Living Australia — 2,077 occurrence records for Sepioteuthis australis, filtered to 1,939 records with valid coordinates. DOI: 10.26197/ala.96c5b17f. Licence: CC BY 4.0.
  • Australian Bureau of Statistics — SA2 digital boundary files, 2021 edition (GDA2020). Licence: CC BY 4.0.

All charts were built with Vega-Lite v5. Geographic data simplified using Mapshaper. Data preparation in Python. Generative AI (Claude, Anthropic) was used to assist with code generation and data processing.
